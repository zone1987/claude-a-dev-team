# Shopware 6 – 3D product models & augmented reality

Upload 3D models (.glb) to products and activate them in the storefront via an AR QR code.
Available from the **Rise plan**. Configuration under **Kataloge > Produkte** (Catalogues > Products) and **Inhalte > Medien** (Content > Media).

## Quick start

1. Open the product → tab **Allgemein** (General) → **Medien** → upload the `.glb` file
2. **Inhalte > Medien** → click the file → **Konfiguration** (Configuration) → enable the AR toggle
3. Record the product size in AR (Shopware does not normalise the size automatically)

Full reference: `3D-PRODUCTS-AR.md`
