# Help & support – where do I find help?

**Source**: https://docs.shopware.com/de/shopware-6-de/erste-schritte/hilfe-aber-wo

## Overview

Shopware offers various support channels which are suitable to differing degrees
depending on the **plan** and the **question**.

---

## Support channels by plan

### Paid plans (Rise, Evolve, Beyond)
- **Shopware Support** is the first point of contact
- Technical help with shop setup, features, errors
- Response time guarantees depending on the contract level
- Create a support ticket: https://account.shopware.com (menu: Support)

### Community Edition (free)
- **Shopware Community Forum**: https://forum.shopware.com
- Peer-to-peer exchange: other shop operators help each other
- No official response time guarantee

---

## Specialised help

### Certified Shopware partners
- Vetted agencies for:
  - Individual development
  - Complete shop builds
  - Complex technical questions (chargeable)
- Find a partner: https://www.shopware.com/de/partner/

### Shopware Community Hub
- URL: https://hub.shopware.com
- Interactive learning paths
- Networking opportunities
- Suitable for self-study and upskilling

## Common support paths

### "I have a technical question about my shop"
1. Search the Shopware Docs (docs.shopware.com)
2. Search the Community Forum for similar questions
3. On a paid plan: create a support ticket in the Shopware Account

### "I have found a bug"
1. Report an issue on GitHub: https://github.com/shopware/shopware
2. Or the Feedback Portal: https://feedback.shopware.com

### "I need individual development"
→ Commission a certified Shopware partner

### "I would like to get to know Shopware better"
→ Community Hub (learning paths) + Docs + Forum
